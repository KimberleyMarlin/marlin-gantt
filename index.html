Consolidation rules
Automatic: exact source record, unique normalised email, or unique normalised phone.
Held for review: conflicting email and phone matches, multiple candidates, or name-only similarities.
Consent: only explicit opt-in or opt-out evidence is recorded. Silence is not consent.
Updates: imports fill gaps and refresh source links. They do not silently overwrite populated identity fields.
Customer files stay in this browser until you approve the import. Do not upload customer exports to GitHub. The preview separates confident matches from records that need a human decision.
CSV and Excel are supported. For Excel, the first visible sheet is used.
